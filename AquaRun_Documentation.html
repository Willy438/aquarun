1. System Summary

AquaRun is a web-based water refill ordering, dispatch, and bottle tracking system designed for local refilling stations. It replaces paper receipts and verbal phone orders with an automated workflow: customers book refills online, station staff manage purification and batch dispatch, riders deliver water with live mobile tracking, and empty gallon swaps and cash collections are reconciled automatically.

2. Tech Stack & APIs Used
Area Technology Purpose
Frontend Vanilla JavaScript (ES Modules), HTML5, CSS3 Lightweight, fast client without heavy frameworks. Direct modular scripts (js/core, js/pages, js/services).
Backend & DB Supabase (PostgreSQL 15+) Relational database, table constraints, triggers, and Row Level Security (RLS) policies.
Authentication Supabase Auth (JWT) Secure customer, staff, rider, and admin logins with role-based routing.
Realtime Sync Supabase Realtime + BroadcastChannel WebSockets for live order updates + browser BroadcastChannel for instant cross-tab sync.

Hosting Vercel / Netlify Edge Continuous deployment from GitHub with custom cache-control headers.
3. User Roles & Responsibilities

Customer

Browses products, selects bottle swap or new container, picks delivery slot, and tracks order status.

Station Staff

Validates orders, manages refill queue, assigns batches to riders, and verifies cash remittances.

Rider

Follows delivery run sheet, delivers gallons, logs returned empty bottles, and submits cash collected.

Admin

Monitors overall sales, hourly demand patterns, slot capacities, product prices, and user accounts.

4. Key Business Logic

1:1 Empty Bottle Swap Ledger

Tracks gallons delivered vs. empties collected per customer. Net held bottles update automatically to prevent bottle loss.

Dual Container Pricing Engine

Refill price (₱30–₱35) if customer returns an empty gallon; full price (refill + ₱150–₱220 container fee) if purchasing new container.

5. End-to-End System Flow

The diagram below illustrates the exact step-by-step lifecycle of an order—from customer checkout to station processing, batch rider assignment, doorstep delivery, and cash remittance closeout:

Customer
Step 1
Places Order Online Status: Submitted
Customer selects water product (Round Gallon, Slim Gallon, or Bottle), specifies whether they are swapping an empty gallon or buying a new container, enters delivery address, and chooses a morning (8–12 AM) or afternoon (1–5 PM) time slot.
↓
Station Staff
Step 2
Confirms Order & Validates Slot Status: Confirmed
Order appears in real time on the Staff Order Queue. Staff verifies slot capacity and delivery address. Staff confirms the order (or adjusts the time slot if needed).
↓
Station Staff
Step 3
Washes, Refills & Packages Gallons Status: Refilling → Ready
Station personnel sanitize the containers, refill them using multi-stage reverse osmosis and UV purification, seal the caps, and click "Mark Ready" on the workbench.
↓
Station Staff
Step 4
Smart Batch Dispatch to Rider Status: Rider Assigned → Out for Delivery
Staff groups ready orders in the same barangay or delivery corridor into a batch run and assigns them to an available rider. The rider receives the delivery stops instantly on their mobile phone.
↓
Rider
Step 5
Doorstep Delivery & Empty Bottle Collection Status: Delivered
Rider arrives at the customer's home, hands over purified gallons, and collects empty swap bottles. On the mobile runsheet, rider logs exact bottles delivered and empties collected (updating the customer's bottle ledger).
↓
Rider
Step 6
Cash Collection & Remittance Submission Payment: Cash on Delivery (COD)
Rider collects cash payment from the customer. At the end of the delivery run, the rider returns to the station and submits their cash turnover report via the mobile remittance screen.
↓
Station Staff
Step 7
Cash Verification & Final Settlement Status: Completed & Settled
Station staff physically counts the cash collected by the rider against the digital total in the Cash & Sales workbench. Staff clicks "Verify & Accept Cash", completing the order and settling the shift accounts.
