SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How AquaRun processes your refill

From selecting your gallon type to bottle ledger reconciliation, every step is coordinated between customer, station staff, and rider.

01

Pick Product & Gallon Option

Select 5-Gallon Round or Slim containers. If you have clean empty containers ready, choose 1:1 Refill Swap. If you need new containers, choose Buy New Container.

  • Round dispenser or slim faucet gallons
  • Transparent price breakdown per gallon
02

Choose Delivery Time Window

Select your delivery date and preferred slot window (Morning 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM or Afternoon 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM). Set your delivery address and specific drop-off notes.

  • Morning & Afternoon slot options
  • Saved delivery addresses with notes
03

Station Refilling & Sanitization

Station staff confirm the order on the station portal, sanitize the containers, refill through multi-stage micro-filtration, and seal caps securely for rider dispatch.

  • Order status updates in real time
  • Clustered neighborhood staging
04

Doorstep Handover & Bottle Ledger

Your rider arrives, delivers the sealed gallons, collects your empty bottles, and receives Cash on Delivery. The rider records bottle counts on their app, syncing your balance instantly.

  • Digital receipt and bottle balance sync
  • Zero surprise fees — flat rate delivery
